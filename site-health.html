Reviewer note

Focusea is a maritime decision-support and education workspace. It does not replace legal advice, class-approved stability software, official port instructions, licensed market data, underwriter approval or navigational decision-making. The product direction is clear: paste a maritime problem, receive a transparent workflow, and know which outputs are verified, estimated or source-dependent.

Useful next steps are backend accounts, verified data integrations, audit logs, official PDF history, and licensed market/AIS feeds. Until those are connected, Focusea keeps sensitive fields labelled and avoids presenting demo values as live facts.
